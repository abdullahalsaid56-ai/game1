# Privacy Policy — Orbit Dash

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

Orbit Dash does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

- The game works fully offline and makes no network requests.
- Your best score and sound setting are stored **only on your device**. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- The game contains no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
- The game does not require an account, and it is safe for players of all ages.

If this policy changes (for example, if leaderboards or ads are added), this page will be updated before the change is released.

Questions: contact the developer at the support email listed on the app's store page.
