Orbit Dash

Tap. Switch orbits. Survive.

Your ship orbits a glowing planet. Tap anywhere to jump between the inner and outer orbit, dodge the red rocks and grab the gold gems. Pick up power-ups, chain gems for a x5 combo, complete missions and unlock new ships. How long can you last?

Free to play. No ads, no accounts, no data collection. Works offline.
