Privacy Policy

Orbit Dash · Last updated October 8, 2026

Orbit Dash does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

  • The game works fully offline and makes no network requests.
  • Your game progress (best score, gems, unlocked ships, missions and sound setting) is stored only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • The game contains no ads, analytics, tracking, or third-party services that collect data.
  • The game does not require an account and is suitable for players of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if online leaderboards or ads are added), this page will be updated before the change is released.
