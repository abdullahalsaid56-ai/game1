Privacy Policy

Orbit Dash · Last updated October 9, 2026

This policy explains what information Orbit Dash ("the game") and its advertising partner collect, and the choices you have.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (best score, gems, unlocked ships, missions, sound setting and whether you bought "Remove Ads") is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers such as your device's advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions (for example, ads viewed and tapped) and app interactions
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data

This information is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and partner policies. We do not receive or store it ourselves.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime under Shop → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iOS, the game asks for permission before using your advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • Buying Remove Ads turns off all ads that appear between games. Ads that you choose to watch for a reward (to continue a run or double your gems) stay available but are always optional.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
