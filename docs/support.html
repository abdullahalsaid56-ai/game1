FAQ

How do I play?
Tap anywhere on the screen to switch between the inner and outer orbit. Avoid red rocks (+1 point for each one you pass) and collect gold gems (+3 points).
What are stations?
Every planet you reach in a run appears on the Galaxy map. Build a station there and it earns gems every hour, even while the game is closed. Each station can only hold about 8 hours of gems, so come back to collect them, and upgrade stations to earn more.
How does the daily reward work?
Open the game once a day to claim a reward. Rewards grow for 7 days in a row (up to 200 gems on day 7). Miss a day and the streak starts again from day 1.
Can I turn off reminders?
Yes. Reminders (stations full, daily reward) can be turned off in your phone's Settings → Notifications → Orbit Dash.
How do I get to the next planet?
Keep dodging until the bar at the top of the screen fills up. A warp gate then appears on one of the orbits: switch into that orbit and fly through it. If you miss it, it comes around again.
How do I beat a boss?
Bosses can't be shot: survive until their health bar at the top runs out. Watch which orbit the Space Worm is in and take the other one. The Laser UFO marks where it will fire with a dashed red line, so don't be in that orbit when you pass it.
The Gas Giant has three orbits. How do I move?
Each tap moves you one orbit in or out. The little arrow next to your ship shows which way the next tap will go; it flips when you reach the innermost or outermost orbit.
What do the power-ups do?
Shield absorbs one hit. Magnet pulls in nearby gems from both orbits. Slow-Mo slows time for a few seconds. 2x doubles all points.
How does the multiplier work?
Every 4 gems in a row raises your multiplier (up to x5). Missing a gem resets it.
How do I unlock new ships?
Gems you collect are saved. Spend them in Skins on the home screen. Completing missions also earns gems.
How do I turn the sound off?
Tap the speaker button in the top-right corner of the title or game-over screen.
Can I pause?
Yes, tap the pause button in the top-left corner. The game also pauses automatically when you leave the app.
How do I remove ads?
Open Shop on the home screen and tap Remove Ads. If you reinstall the game or get a new phone, tap Restore purchases in the Shop.
Can I change my ad privacy choices?
Yes. If you're in a region that requires consent, tap Ad privacy choices in the Shop. See the privacy policy for details.
I lost my best score.
Your best score, gems and unlocked ships are saved only on your device, so it is reset if you uninstall the app or switch devices.
